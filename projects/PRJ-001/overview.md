## Purpose

Replace an unreliable ISP-supplied router with a setup that gives stable Wi-Fi
across two floors, a wired connection for the desk, and a separate guest
network. Done means a week of normal use with no drop-outs and the old router
returned.

## Established direction

- **OWNER DECISION — 2026-03-02:** Use a two-unit mesh system rather than a
  single higher-power router, because the second floor is behind concrete.
- **OWNER DECISION — 2026-03-02:** Budget ceiling of 200 (local currency) for
  hardware; cabling quoted separately.

## Requirements and constraints

- Wired gigabit to the desk; Wi-Fi for everything else.
- Guest network isolated from work devices.
- No changes to the ISP account without the owner present.
